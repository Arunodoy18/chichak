our story

Every Roll Tells A Story

Chikchak is a Borehamwood neighborhood spot serving fresh kosher sushi, vibrant poké bowls, and wok-tossed noodles that blend Japanese tradition with Mediterranean ingredients.

Founded to bring quality sushi to north London, we focus on honest flavors, fresh fish, and welcoming hospitality every day.
